7. ORTHOGONAL STATE MODEL

NON utilizzare un unico state per rappresentare tutto.

Separare:

LIFECYCLE OPERATIONAL MODE HEALTH APPROVAL DEPLOYMENT ROLE

Esempio Model:

Lifecycle: EXPERIMENTAL CANDIDATE APPROVED RETIRED

Mode: RESEARCH BACKTEST SIMULATION SHADOW CANARY LIVE CLOSING_ONLY

Health: HEALTHY DEGRADED FAILED FROZEN

Approval: PENDING APPROVED EXPIRED REVOKED

Deployment: NOT_DEPLOYED STAGED ACTIVE ROLLED_BACK FAILED

Role: NONE CHALLENGER CHAMPION

Questi assi sono indipendenti.

8. STATE COMPATIBILITY MATRIX

Creare una matrice machine-checkable:

Lifecycle × Operational Mode × Health × Approval × Deployment × Role

Risultato:

VALID INVALID

Esempi:

EXPERIMENTAL × LIVE → INVALID.

APPROVED × LIVE × APPROVED × ACTIVE → VALID.

RETIRED × LIVE → INVALID.

HEALTH=FAILED × LIVE → INVALID.

APPROVAL=EXPIRED × LIVE → INVALID.

CHAMPION × NOT_DEPLOYED → INVALID.

La matrice deve essere versionata e testata.

14. RISK MONOTONICITY

Regole matematiche obbligatorie:

Higher uncertainty → risk cannot increase.

Worse liquidity → allowed exposure cannot increase.

Higher drawdown → risk budget cannot increase.

Worse model health → risk cannot increase.

Higher execution risk → position size cannot increase.

Higher concentration → correlated exposure cannot increase.

15. RISK BUDGET MONOTONICITY

remaining_budget available_new_risk maximum_exposure
