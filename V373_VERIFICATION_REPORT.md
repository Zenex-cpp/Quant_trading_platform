## Verification
- Full automated suite: **PASS — 691 tests** (680 pre-existing + 11 new;
  zero regressions).
- No-intent run stays flat (no fills, zero equity drift): PASS.
- Buy-and-hold mark-to-market equity matches hand-computed value: PASS.
- Fee and slippage reduce realized equity in the expected direction: PASS.
- Latency-offset delays a fill to the correct later bar's price: PASS.
- Out-of-order market events raise `OutOfOrderEventError`: PASS.

- Symbol-mismatched intents are rejected: PASS.

  an identical `state_hash` and identical fill sequence: PASS.
- Under a `STOCHASTIC_BUT_SEEDED` execution model, two runs with the same
  seed match exactly, and the check also confirms the scenario is not
  vacuous (some bars fill, some don't, at p=0.5 over 20 bars): PASS.
- `ExecutionModelConfig` rejects negative cost parameters, a missing
  version, and a fill-probability/determinism-class mismatch: PASS.
- A non-finite OHLCV value (e.g. `NaN` close) is rejected at construction:
  PASS.

## Evidence
`V373_BACKTEST_ENGINE_EVIDENCE.json` has `source_kind=INTERNAL` and scope
`APPLICATION_BACKTEST_ENGINE_DETERMINISM`, generated by actually running
`run_v373_backtest_determinism.py` (not hand-written) against two scenarios:
an `EXACT` 15-bar run and a `STOCHASTIC_BUT_SEEDED` 30-bar run at
`fill_probability=0.5`. Both report `matched`/`same_seed_reproducible =
true`, and the stochastic scenario confirms a genuine mix of fills and
misses rather than an accidental all-or-nothing result.

## Evidence boundary
This evidence proves the backtest engine replays deterministically under
its own declared rules. It does **not** prove:
- backtest/live parity (§85) — no live runner exists yet to share the
  injected stage callables with;
- STRESS simulation mode (§87) — declared, not implemented;
- realistic execution microstructure (§86) — no order-book depth, partial
  fills, or venue-specific rejection codes;
- resumable/incremental replay (§88) — only full-run determinism is proven.

These are open follow-on items, not claimed as closed by this release.
